What the page has to do

Positioning

  • Show technical depth through detail, not loud claims: calm, black-and-white, lots of air. Reference: sdg.la/shopify-integrations.
  • Focus: complex integrations: ERP, custom API, inventory sync, POS.
  • One to three strong case blocks instead of ten small ones.
  • Give a German-speaking buyer a person in the region. Our site has no other human presence today.

How Alexander appears

  • Our partner and contact for the DACH region: photo, name, role, one first-person quote. Format reference: what.digital/de/shopify-erp-integrationen.
  • No direct email, phone or calendar of his. Every CTA goes to our contact form; we handle the enquiry and bring Alexander into the call when needed.
  • No logos of his clients (Jungheinrich, EnBW, …). Those are his projects, not ours.
