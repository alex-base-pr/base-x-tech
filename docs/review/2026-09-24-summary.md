## Verdict

The new service copy is strong (≈7/10 as writing; FAQs are the best part; one clear position — *Shopify for stores
where the build is the hard part*). Conversion overall ≈4.5/10 because the **legacy layer** around the new copy
contradicts it: old home copy, the $2200 "for Ukraine" pricing card, the generic "Approach" block on every page,
an English USD form with a pre-selected $2k budget, anonymous proof and no answer to "who are you, where are you,
who does the work". Roughly 70% of the fix is copy/structure — no redesign needed.

Persona outcome: Katrin (DE, Xentral) — no · Tom (UK, configurator) — maybe ~40% · Marta (EU, Magento) — likely ~60% ·
Lukas (CH, white-label) — maybe ~50%.
