Case
Label: Proven in practice
Client: FRESH MARKET
Tags: Private App · Inventory Sync · POS Integration · Automation
The challenge: Fresh Market sells the same inventory through its Shopify store and physical locations. Without a shared inventory flow, an item sold in-store could remain available online — creating overselling, cancelled orders and manual stock corrections.
The challenge was to make both channels operate against the same inventory, so every sale is reflected across the business automatically.
What we built: We built a connected commerce system that keeps the online store, physical locations and inventory in sync through a custom integration layer. A private app handles the business-specific logic, while automated data flows keep stock availability updated across channels as sales happen.
Instead of managing online and offline inventory separately, the store operates around one connected flow — with Shopify, POS and inventory data working together behind the scenes.
Key outcomes:
- Private App — Custom logic built around the store’s inventory and sales workflow, connecting systems where a standard integration wasn’t enough.
- Inventory Sync — Stock availability stays aligned between online and physical stores as products are sold across either channel.
- POS Integration — In-store sales feed back into the connected inventory flow, so offline transactions are reflected online automatically.
- Automation — Routine inventory updates happen automatically, reducing the need for manual stock adjustments between channels.
