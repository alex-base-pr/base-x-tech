FAQ
faq.h2 → Questions we get asked

Eight rows. Answers must render in the HTML whether a row is open or closed — if the accordion fetches them on click they are invisible to crawlers and to AI answer engines, and the section stops doing the job it was added for. Confirm with Dima at build time.
Can you customise Shopify checkout?
Yes, on Shopify Plus. Checkout validation, discount logic and delivery rules are built with Shopify Functions and checkout extensions rather than workarounds. On standard plans the options are much narrower, and we will tell you which of your requirements are not possible before you commit to anything.
Do we need Shopify Plus?
Only for specific things. Plus is required for checkout customisation, B2B company accounts and higher API limits. If none of your requirements need those, a standard plan is usually enough and we will say so rather than sell you the upgrade.
Can you build a product configurator on Shopify?
Yes, and we have. A configurator built into the storefront handles options, dependencies and made-to-order ranges that variants alone cannot express. Prime EVA is the example: a custom configurator running inside their Shopify store rather than as a bolted-on app.
Can one store serve wholesale and retail?
Yes. Shopify B2B runs company accounts, price lists and payment terms alongside your retail catalogue, so you are not maintaining two stores that drift apart. We have built this, though that client's work is not public.
When does headless Shopify make sense?
Less often than it gets recommended. Headless is worth it when you need a front end Shopify's theme layer cannot deliver, or when the storefront serves more than one channel. It costs more to build and more to maintain, so if a well-built theme does the job we will tell you that instead.
Can you work with our existing theme, or is it a rebuild?
Usually your existing theme. We audit what it can carry, extend it where that holds up, and only recommend a rebuild when the theme is fighting what you need. A rebuild you did not need is the most expensive thing on a Shopify project.
How long does a custom Shopify build take?
Most builds run 4 to 16 weeks, depending on the integrations and how much is custom rather than configured. We scope and estimate before writing code, so the timeline is agreed at the start.
What happens after launch?
We stay on it. We watch the store under real traffic, fix what surfaces in the first weeks, and keep it working through Shopify's API version changes. Handing over a login and disappearing is where most of the problems we get called in to fix started.
