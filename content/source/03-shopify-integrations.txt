FAQ
faq.h2 → Questions we get asked

Eight rows. Answers must render in the HTML whether a row is open or closed — if the accordion fetches them on click they are invisible to crawlers and to AI answer engines. Confirm with Dima at build time.
Can you connect Shopify to our ERP?
Yes. Orders, stock levels and invoices move between Shopify and your ERP without anyone re-typing them. We work through the API your ERP exposes, and where it exposes nothing usable we build the connector ourselves rather than telling you it cannot be done.
What if our system has no Shopify app?
That is most of the work we do. Off-the-shelf apps cover common systems; the one that runs your business often predates them or was built in-house. We connect those directly through their API, or build a private Shopify app to sit between them.
How do you stop stock going out of sync?
By deciding which system owns the number before anything is built. Stock drifts when two systems both think they are authoritative, so we set direction and frequency for every field, then run real orders through it, including the ones that fail, before it touches live data.
Can you automate order routing and fulfilment?
Yes. For a UK gifting brand we built a production engine that turns each order into print-ready files, batches them and routes them to the production queue, plus a fulfilment layer that generates shipping codes, syncs couriers in real time and schedules pickups twice a day.
Which ERPs and CRMs have you worked with?
ERP, CRM, PIM and fulfilment systems across the common categories, connected through whatever API they expose. Several clients run systems with no public integration at all, and for those we have built custom connectors for the specific use rather than moving the business onto a different tool.
How long does an integration take?
It depends on how many systems and how clean their data is. A single well-documented connection is measured in weeks. The UK gifting brand's three-system build, covering configurator, production and fulfilment, took four to six months. We scope it before quoting.
Who maintains it after launch?
We do, if you want us to. Integrations break when either side changes: an API version is deprecated, a field is renamed, a system is upgraded. We monitor and alert on the connections we build, because a silent integration failure costs more than a loud one.
Can you integrate with our warehouse or 3PL?
Yes. We have built barcode scanning and routing logic into warehouse operations, and courier integrations that generate labels, push tracking back to the store and schedule collections. Nova Poshta Connect is the public version of that work, listed on the Shopify App Store.
