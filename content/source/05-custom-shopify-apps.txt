FAQ
faq.h2 → Questions we get asked

Answers must render in the HTML whether a row is open or closed. Confirm with Dima.
Can you build a public Shopify app for the App Store?
Yes. Two we can name publicly are live there: Nova Poshta Connect, which is ours, and Mo: Story Slider Navigation, built for a partner agency. Most of what we build is private apps for one merchant, which never appear in the store by design.

This answer now does three jobs — proves App Store delivery twice, explains the count without sounding defensive, and sells the private-app capability on the way past.
What is the difference between a public and a private app?
A public app is listed on the App Store and any merchant can install it. A private app exists for one store and does exactly what that business needs. If the workflow is specific to you, private is usually cheaper and faster than bending a public app to fit.
Can you automate something our team does by hand?
That is most of what we build. For a UK gifting brand we shipped a configurator as a private app, plus a production engine that turns each order into print-ready files and routes them to the queue. Work that used to be manual now happens on order.
How long does a Shopify app take to build?
A single-purpose private app is measured in weeks. Something with production logic, external systems or App Store review behind it runs longer. The gifting brand's three-system build took four to six months. We scope before quoting so you are not guessing.
Who owns the app and the code?
You do, for anything we build for you. Private apps and custom code are yours, including the repository. A public app is a different question and worth settling in the contract before it is built rather than after it has users.
What happens when Shopify changes its API?
Shopify deprecates API versions on a schedule, so an app left alone will eventually break. We keep the apps we build working through those changes. That is maintenance of our own code, and we would not claim to maintain an app we have never seen.
Can you build checkout or discount logic?
Yes, using Shopify Functions and checkout extensions rather than workarounds. Checkout specifically needs Shopify Plus. Discount and delivery logic have more room on standard plans, and we will tell you which of your rules are possible before you commit.
Can you take over an app someone else built?
Sometimes, and we audit it before saying yes. Taking on a codebase without knowing what is in it is how you inherit someone else's problem. If it is maintainable we will say so. If rebuilding costs less than untangling it, we will say that instead.

The last two answers exist because of your own objection. "Upgrades and maintenance" of apps you didn't build was the thing you called fraudulent, so the page now says exactly where the line is. Naming a limit is what makes the rest credible.
