Spec for Ira
	•	Section label + one H2 for the section.
	•	Eight accordion rows, same pattern as the process section already in the frame.
	•	Question is an H3. Longest here is 57 characters, so design for ~60 on one or two lines.
	•	Answer is a paragraph, 36–55 words. Design for roughly 350 characters.
	•	First row open by default is fine; the rest closed.
	•	Answers must render in the HTML whether the row is open or closed. If the accordion loads answers only on click, the content is invisible to crawlers and to AI answer engines, and the whole section stops working for the purpose it was added for. Worth confirming with Dima at build time.

Web Design — FAQ
faq.h2 → Questions we get asked
Do you design and build, or design only?
Both, and the same team does both. Your design goes to the developers who built the last one, so nothing is lost between the file and the store. We do take design-only work, and even then the design is made to be built: real components, real states, and no decisions that fall apart in code.
Can you work with our existing Shopify theme?
Yes. Most of our design work starts from a theme that already exists rather than a blank file. We audit what the theme can carry, design inside those limits where that is sensible, and tell you which changes need theme development rather than a design adjustment.
What do we get at the end?
Figma files with the components and states, a design system you can reuse, clickable prototypes for the flows that matter, and specifications the developers build from. If we are building it, you also get the store. If someone else is, the handoff is written for them rather than for us.
Do you design for products people configure or compare?
That is most of what we do. Configurators, variant-heavy catalogues, B2B ordering and multi-step selection all need design decisions that a single product page template cannot carry. We build these too, so the design accounts for what is actually possible on Shopify.

Link "configurators" to /pages/shopify-development.
Do you build design systems or just pages?
Both, and the system is usually the cheaper choice. A set of components and rules your team can reuse costs more up front than a handful of pages and stops the store drifting apart every time someone adds a section. For a growing catalogue it pays back quickly.
Can you design for German or other EU markets?
Yes. We run a multi-language site ourselves and build multi-market Shopify setups, so the design accounts for what changes between languages: German text runs longer than English and breaks layouts that were only ever tested in one language. Copy in German is written by a native speaker, not translated by us.

The strongest question on the page for your target market. It is also the only one that names a limitation, which is what makes the rest credible.
How long does a Shopify store design take?
A storefront design is usually a 3 to 8 week engagement, depending on how many page types you need and how many review rounds you want. We scope it before starting so the timeline is agreed rather than discovered.
What does Shopify store design cost?
It depends on the number of page types and whether we are building it as well as designing it. We scope every project before quoting, and we will tell you if what you need is smaller than the work we take on.


Shopify Development — FAQ
faq.h2 → Questions we get asked
Can you customise Shopify checkout?
Yes, on Shopify Plus. Checkout validation, discount logic and delivery rules are built with Shopify Functions and checkout extensions rather than workarounds. On standard plans the options are much narrower, and we will tell you which of your requirements are not possible before you commit to anything.
Do we need Shopify Plus?
Only for specific things. Plus is required for checkout customisation, B2B company accounts and higher API limits. If none of your requirements need those, a standard plan is usually enough and we will say so rather than sell you the upgrade.
Can you build a product configurator on Shopify?
Yes, and we have. A configurator built into the storefront handles options, dependencies and made-to-order ranges that variants alone cannot express. Naya.tech is the example: a custom configurator running inside their Shopify store rather than as a bolted-on app.
Can one store serve wholesale and retail?
Yes. Shopify B2B runs company accounts, price lists and payment terms alongside your retail catalogue, so you are not maintaining two stores that drift apart. We have built this, though that client's work is not public.
When does headless Shopify make sense?
Less often than it gets recommended. Headless is worth it when you need a front end Shopify's theme layer cannot deliver, or when the storefront serves more than one channel. It costs more to build and more to maintain, so if a well-built theme does the job we will tell you that instead.

Can you work with our existing theme, or is it a rebuild?
Usually your existing theme. We audit what it can carry, extend it where that holds up, and only recommend a rebuild when the theme is fighting what you need. A rebuild you did not need is the most expensive thing on a Shopify project.
How long does a custom Shopify build take?
Most builds run 4 to 16 weeks, depending on the integrations and how much is custom rather than configured. We scope and estimate before writing code, so the timeline is agreed at the start.
What happens after launch?
We stay on it. We watch the store under real traffic, fix what surfaces in the first weeks, and keep it working through Shopify's API version changes. Handing over a login and disappearing is where most of the problems we get called in to fix started.
